About Vakmira

One specialist who understands both the language and the audit.

Vakmira was founded by Manish Kumar Tibrewal, who has worked in pharmaceutical audits for 14 years. He began as a translator and now also leads pre-audits, audit preparation and compliance consulting.

He holds a postgraduate degree in Spanish from the University of Delhi, where he was a gold medallist, and a PGDM in business development from NMIMS.

Vakmira supports Indian manufacturers preparing to enter or be certified in Latin American markets, and Latin American distributors who want to source from India.
